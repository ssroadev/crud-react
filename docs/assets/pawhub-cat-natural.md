## Prompt utilizado

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: square photographic portrait to be displayed inside a circular crop on the PawHub login page. Input image: the original PawHub design is the identity and pose reference for its brown tabby cat only. Create a much more natural, believable photograph of this same brown tabby cat, retaining the characteristic forehead stripes, tan muzzle, green eyes, and gently tilted head looking slightly upward. Show a relaxed real domestic cat with normal feline eye proportions, natural green irises and realistic pupils, soft imperfect fur and fine whiskers. Soft diffuse daylight, restrained contrast, true photographic detail, no oversharpening, no glossy or airbrushed finish, no cartoon or uncanny oversized eyes. Frame the full head, both ears, neck and upper chest, centered in a square with 12 percent breathing space above the ears and on both sides so a circular CSS crop does not clip them. The softly out-of-focus natural background blends warm honey and muted gold near the top right into sage and olive green at the lower corners. Keep background tonal transitions gentle and subdued, matching a warm gold-to-green webpage gradient, with realistic photographic depth, not a flat beige disc. Deliver the photograph only, no designed circle or frame, no white border, no text, no logo, no card, no blue. Keep the cat's appearance faithful to the original reference.
